How scores are calculated

Each metric ranges from 0 to 100. Amount compares the payment with the median of up to 50 prior successful transactions. Timing compares its UTC hour with that history. Frequency counts payments in the preceding hour. Recipient measures familiarity. Reports combines this payment’s report with distinct customers reporting payments to the same IBAN. Daily limits compares outgoing payment count and EUR total with the account’s thresholds at the payment time.

Misalignment weights amount 35%, timing 15%, frequency 15%, recipient 15% and reports 20%. Daily limit usage adds up to 25 points; the total is capped at 100. Reaching a limit does not reject payments. Higher scores prioritize review; they are not probabilities of fraud. Limited history is shown when inspecting a payment.
